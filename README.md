# PLG Onboarding Playbook

A working checklist for in-app onboarding, activation and retention in product-led SaaS. Short on theory, long on things you can ship this week.

Written alongside [PLG OS](https://www.plgos.com), which gives product-led SaaS teams in-app onboarding, gamification and loyalty experiences.

## 1. Define activation first
- [ ] Write down the one action that predicts a new user will still be around in 30 days (the "aha" action).
- [ ] Measure time-to-activation and the share of signups who reach it in their first session and first week.
- [ ] Every onboarding step below should move one of those two numbers.

## 2. First session
- [ ] Ask at most 1 to 3 questions at signup, and use the answers to change what the user sees next.
- [ ] Replace empty states with a sample project, template or one-click demo data.
- [ ] Show a short checklist (3 to 5 items) that ends at the activation action, not at "complete your profile".
- [ ] Let people skip and come back. Never trap them in a tour.

## 3. Progress and motivation
- [ ] Show visible progress (a bar or count) on the onboarding checklist.
- [ ] Celebrate the activation action the moment it happens.
- [ ] Use streaks, points or badges only where they reward real product use, not vanity clicks.

## 4. Bring people back
- [ ] Trigger emails or in-app messages from behaviour (stalled on step 2), not from the calendar.
- [ ] Surface the next most valuable feature after activation, one at a time.
- [ ] Reward referrals and repeat use with something users actually want (credits, extra limits, early access).

## 5. Measure and iterate
- [ ] Track funnel drop-off for each onboarding step.
- [ ] Change one step at a time and compare activation rate before and after.
- [ ] Interview five users who dropped off and five who activated every month.
